02 — Practice

Objects are not announced; they are put into use. Each begins as a condition to solve and ends as something held, pressed, worn or lived beside.

The archive collects two decades of product and industrial design across four territories — computing, lifestyle, consumer objects, and larger brand projects. The work moves between the precision of gaming hardware and the patience of furniture and jewellery.

Recognition has followed: Red Dot, iF, G-Mark and Golden Pin, alongside CES Innovation, Computex d&i and Taiwan Excellence honours. The awards are records of a practice, not its purpose.

Photographs are kept in black & white until met by attention.
Colour returns to an object only when it is looked at.
